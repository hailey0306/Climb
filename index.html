Don't climb on wet sandstone

Moisture weakens the sandstone. Local references recommend waiting roughly 24–72 hours depending on rain, temperature, sun, and shade; don't climb until the rock and surrounding sand are truly dry. The clock alone is not a guarantee.

Respect access: No camping in Moe's Valley or Mario Land. Stay on established routes and pack out trash.
